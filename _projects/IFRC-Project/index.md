title: FIRST Robotics
description: FIRST Robotics is an engineering competition requiring teams to design and build a functional robot within strict time and performance constraints to perform a certain task or mission. The goal during my time was to build a robot capable of launching dodgeballs into various targets of ranging score. This experience taught me how to design for reliability under stress, collaborate effectively, and make practical engineering tradeoffs. I also frequently had to alternate between working on mechanical design problems and electrical problems because of a shortage of students. This exposed me to fast paced, dynamic work conditions, but built fundamental collaborative and project management skills.
skills: 
- Fusion Design
- Machining (Band saw, Lathe)
- Electro-Mechanical Integration
- Rapid Design Iteration
- Collaboration
- Deadline Management
