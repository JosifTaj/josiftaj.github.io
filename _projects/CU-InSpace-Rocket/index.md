title: CU InSpace Rocket
description: CU InSpace is a student rocketry team focused on designing, building, and testing high power rockets. The team splits work amongst aerostructures, avionics, propulsion, and software subteams working toward integrated flight systems. On the aerostructures team, I'm currently researching dynamic stability, what factors affect our rocket's performance, and how to optimize its flight. I also design and refine mechanical components and structural assemblies for high-power student rockets. I've iterated designs using SolidWorks CAD, shop tools, and physical test feedback while collaborating across subteams to ensure system-level integration. The first rocket of the images below is from the 2026 Launch Canada event. It reached a max altitude of over 39,000 ft and hit a max speed of Mach 1.89!
